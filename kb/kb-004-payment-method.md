# KB-004: Updating your payment method
Only workspace owners and billing admins can change billing details.

1. Go to Settings > Billing > Payment method.
2. Click "Replace card" and enter the new card details. We accept Visa, Mastercard, and American Express.
3. Annual Enterprise plans can switch to invoicing (bank transfer, 30-day terms) by contacting billing.

Failed payments: if a renewal charge fails, we retry automatically after 3, 5, and 7 days and email the
billing contact each time. The workspace stays fully active during this period. After the final failed
retry the workspace becomes read-only until payment succeeds; no data is deleted.

Never send card numbers to support by email or chat. Support staff will never ask for them.
