# KB-006: Refund and cancellation policy
Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time under Settings > Billing > Cancel plan. Cancellation takes effect
at the end of the current billing period; monthly payments are not refunded.

Annual plans: a full refund is available if requested within 14 days of the initial purchase or annual
renewal. After 14 days, annual plans are not refundable but can be set to not renew.

Duplicate charges or billing errors are always corrected once verified by the billing team.

All refund requests are reviewed and approved by the billing team, not by frontline support. Support
agents log the request and the billing team responds within 3 business days. Service credits for
downtime are governed by the SLA in Enterprise contracts and are handled by the account manager.
