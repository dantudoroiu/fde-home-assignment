# KB-012: Data deletion and privacy requests (GDPR / CCPA)
Brightdesk acts as a data processor for customer data in workspaces and as a controller for account data.

Workspace owners can delete the entire workspace under Settings > Workspace > Delete. Data is removed from
production immediately and from backups within 30 days.

Requests from individuals (data subject access or deletion requests) must be verified. Frontline support
does not process these directly: they are forwarded to the privacy team at privacy@brightdesk.example,
which confirms receipt within 2 business days and completes requests within the statutory deadline (one
month under GDPR).

A Data Processing Agreement (DPA) is available for all paid plans; Enterprise customers can request
custom terms through their account manager. Our subprocessor list is published at
brightdesk.example/legal/subprocessors.
