# KB-005: Understanding your invoice and proration
Invoices are available under Settings > Billing > Invoices and are emailed to the billing contact. Each
invoice has a number in the format INV-12345.

Proration: when you add seats or upgrade mid-cycle, you are charged immediately for the remaining part of
the current billing period, so an invoice can be smaller or larger than your usual amount. Removing seats
takes effect at the next renewal; it does not generate a credit for the current period.

Taxes: VAT or sales tax is added based on the billing address. To add a VAT ID, go to Settings > Billing >
Company details. Invoices already issued cannot be changed retroactively, but future invoices will
include the VAT ID.

If you believe an invoice is wrong, reply with the invoice number and what you expected to be charged.
The billing team reviews disputed invoices within 3 business days.
